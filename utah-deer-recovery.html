🌡️ Why Thermal Works in UT

Thermal cameras detect the heat signature of deer even through thick brush and timber. Early snow aids recovery. Canyon country offers unique thermal opportunities.

🌲 Utah Hunting Terrain

High mountain forests, desert canyons, sagebrush flats, and alpine meadows. Each terrain type presents unique tracking challenges that thermal drones overcome.

Traditional blood tracking becomes difficult or impossible in many Utah conditions. Thermal drone recovery offers a reliable alternative when visual tracking fails.
