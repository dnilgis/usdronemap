Tips for Lost Pet Owners

What to do while arranging a drone search

🔔 Don't Chase

Scared pets run. Leave food and familiar items at the last known location instead of pursuing.

📢 Alert Neighbors

Post on local social media, Nextdoor, and lost pet groups. More eyes help.

🌙 Search at Dawn/Dusk

Pets often move during low-light hours. Thermal works best then too.

📞 Call Shelters

Contact local animal control and shelters daily. Provide photos and descriptions.
