Frequently Asked Questions

Drone deer recovery uses thermal imaging cameras to detect heat signatures. The drone flies over the search area and locates deer even through thick cover, at night, or when traditional tracking fails.
Services typically cost $150-400 per search depending on location and time. Many pilots offer flat rates or "no find, no fee" options.
Contact a pilot as soon as traditional tracking fails. Thermal works best 2-12 hours after the shot when there's still a temperature difference. Don't wait more than 24 hours.
Thermal works best in cooler conditions (below 60°F). In warm weather, searches before sunrise or after sunset are most effective.
